## Users

Healthcare professionals and the medical facilities they work in. The About page names the audience as "healthcare professionals worldwide", across all resource settings. The homepage speaks to facilities (radiology centers, hospitals, clinics) choosing systems such as RADex and MEND. The mobile apps (ABGen, Atria) are for individual physicians. Inferred from the site copy; the user pointed to the About page as the source.

## Product Purpose

Syvursoft is the parent company. It builds medical software that is practical at the point of care: fast enough for pressure, structured enough for operations, clear enough to improve decisions. Success is a visitor understanding who builds this and why, and moving on to a product (RADex, MEND, Atria, ABGen) or to contact.

## Positioning

Founded by Dr. Waleed Sheha, an emergency and critical care physician, after years in busy emergency rooms and ICUs. The products come from someone who worked at the center of events and lived the whole experience, not from a software vendor guessing at clinical work.

## Operating Context

- Emergency rooms and ICUs: quick decisions with complex patient data, blood gas results above all (the origin of ABGen).
- Radiology departments: RIS, PACS, DICOM viewing, reporting, scheduling, billing (RADex).
- Whole facilities: departments, teams, maintenance, audit, controlled from one system that simulates the facility (MEND).
- Echocardiography: turning measurements into a written, branded PDF report on a phone (Atria).
